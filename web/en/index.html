Kolorist

Build colour palettes across colour spaces – with perceptually even gradients, contrast checking, ICC profiles and export to the tools you already use.

Features

Kolorist is designed for iPhone first. On iPad and Mac you get more room – in landscape on iPad the colour swatches sit beside the tools – and on the Mac an eyedropper that picks colours from anywhere on screen.

  • Every colour space

    Edit colours in OKLCH, OKLab, CIE LCH, CIELab, HSB, HSL, RGB and CMYK – with Display P3 as the wide gamut.

    • Display P3 side by side with sRGB, Adobe RGB, Rec. 2020, ProPhoto RGB, CMYK or any installed ICC profile
    • Enter CMYK or RGB directly in a chosen ICC profile
    • Save a colour in its colour model or in the profile – exports follow the format
    • Warnings when a colour is out of gamut
    • Optionally limit new colours to a chosen profile
    • Clean CMYK values: grey components move to black (UCR/GCR), with as few inks as possible

  • ICC profiles

    Import your own profiles (.icc and .icm). They live in the app’s iCloud Drive folder and follow you to your other devices.

    • Convert between profiles with a chosen rendering intent
    • Compare rendering intents with ΔE2000

  • Gradients

    Create transitions between two colours in equal perceptual steps in OKLab, with lighter and darker rows.

    • Export as a CSS gradient in oklab with an sRGB fallback
    • Linear, radial or conic
    • Save whole gradients in Palettes and open them again
    • Touch and hold a tone to save it

  • Tones and harmonies

    Lighter and darker steps, tone scales from 50 to 950, and colour harmonies:

    • Complementary, split complementary, analogous and even distribution with a chosen count
    • On an OKLCH, CIE LCH, HSL or RYB colour wheel
    • Saturation and lightness for the whole harmony, shown right in the colour wheel
    • Lighter and darker tones in equal or easing steps

  • Pick colours

    • Camera: tap to pick, pinch to zoom, macro focus and torch – a light panel on the Mac screen
    • Photos and images: zoom and dominant colours
    • Screen eyedropper on Mac

  • Apple Intelligence on device

    • From value words to a palette, grounded in a knowledge base of more than a hundred colour concepts – nature, materials, styles and values
    • Describe a colour – “deep ocean blue”, “dusty pink” – and see it in Studio
    • Adjust with free text – “warmer”, “more exclusive”
    • Name colours and get a critique of your palette

    Everything runs on device. Nothing leaves it.

  • Accessibility

    • WCAG 2.2 contrast: AA and AAA, large text and graphics
    • Auto-fix that adjusts the colour until it passes
    • Contrast matrix for the whole palette
    • Compare colours with ΔE2000
    • See the palette with protan, deutan and tritan deficiencies and achromatopsia, at any severity – and which colours become hard to tell apart
    • Camera with a colour vision filter: see your surroundings as they may appear with each deficiency

  • Palettes and iCloud

    Collect colours in palettes, save single colours and whole gradients. Name them, and drag colours between palettes.

    Everything syncs through your own private iCloud.

  • Export

    Adobe ASE, Photoshop ACO, Figma variables (DTCG JSON), Tokens Studio, SVG swatches, CSS, Design Tokens, GIMP GPL, SwiftUI and hex list.

    Colours are exported in the format they were saved in – for example CMYK to Adobe.

    Copy to … Figma, Illustrator, InDesign, Photoshop, Sketch and Affinity, Pages, Keynote and Numbers, CSS or SwiftUI – in the format each app accepts. Works for single colours and whole palettes.

    On Mac, drag swatches straight into other apps.

    Import: colour libraries in ASE, ACO and ACB (Adobe Color Book) – and find the nearest named tone in the library with ΔE2000, for example in a free colour chart such as Freetone.

  • Siri and Shortcuts

    • Create a palette from value words
    • Create a gradient
    • Convert a colour
    • Check contrast
    • Describe a colour

  • Open about methods

    Every part of the app shows the methods it builds on – OKLab, CSS Color 4, CIEDE2000, WCAG, colour vision simulation, ICC – with source and explanation. What was developed for Kolorist is marked as such.

Privacy

No account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer collects no data. Your palettes live on your device and in your private iCloud, and Apple Intelligence runs on device.
