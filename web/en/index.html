Kolorist

Build color palettes across color spaces – with perceptually even gradients, contrast checking, ICC profiles and export to the tools you already use.

Features

Kolorist is designed for iPhone first. On iPad and Mac you get more room – and on the Mac an eyedropper that picks colors from anywhere on screen.

  • Every color space

    Edit colors in OKLCH, OKLab, CIE LCH, CIELab, HSB, HSL, RGB and CMYK – with Display P3 as the wide gamut.

    • Display P3 side by side with sRGB, Adobe RGB, CMYK or any installed ICC profile
    • Enter CMYK or RGB directly in a chosen ICC profile
    • Save a color in its color model or in the profile – exports follow the format
    • Warnings when a color is out of gamut
    • Optionally limit new colors to a chosen profile

  • ICC profiles

    Import your own profiles (.icc and .icm). They live in the app’s iCloud Drive folder and follow you to your other devices.

    • Convert between profiles with a chosen rendering intent
    • Compare rendering intents with ΔE2000

  • Gradients

    Create transitions between two colors in equal perceptual steps in OKLab, with lighter and darker rows.

    • Export as a CSS gradient in oklab with an sRGB fallback
    • Linear, radial or conic
    • Save whole gradients in Palettes and open them again
    • Touch and hold a tone to save it

  • Tones and harmonies

    Lighter and darker steps, tone scales from 50 to 950, and color harmonies:

    • Complementary, split complementary, analogous and even distribution with a chosen count
    • On an OKLCH, CIE LCH, HSL or RYB color wheel
    • Saturation and lightness for the whole harmony, shown right in the color wheel
    • Lighter and darker tones in equal or easing steps

  • Pick colors

    • Camera: tap to pick, pinch to zoom, macro focus and torch – a light panel on the Mac screen
    • Photos and images: zoom and dominant colors
    • Screen eyedropper on Mac

  • Apple Intelligence on device

    • From value words to a palette, grounded in a knowledge base of more than a hundred color concepts – nature, materials, styles and values
    • Describe a color – “deep ocean blue”, “dusty pink” – and see it in Studio
    • Adjust with free text – “warmer”, “more exclusive”
    • Name colors and get a critique of your palette

    Everything runs on device. Nothing leaves it.

  • Accessibility

    • WCAG 2.2 contrast: AA and AAA, large text and graphics
    • Auto-fix that adjusts the color until it passes
    • Contrast matrix for the whole palette
    • Compare colors with ΔE2000

  • Palettes and iCloud

    Collect colors in palettes, save single colors and whole gradients. Name them, and drag colors between palettes.

    Everything syncs through your own private iCloud.

  • Export

    Adobe ASE, Photoshop ACO, Figma variables (DTCG JSON), Tokens Studio, SVG swatches, CSS, Design Tokens, GIMP GPL, SwiftUI and hex list.

    Colors are exported in the format they were saved in – for example CMYK to Adobe.

    Copy and paste the way you’re used to on each platform.

  • Siri and Shortcuts

    • Create a palette from value words
    • Create a gradient
    • Convert a color
    • Check contrast
    • Describe a color

Privacy

No account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer collects no data. Your palettes live on your device and in your private iCloud, and Apple Intelligence runs on device.
