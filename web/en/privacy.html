Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

In short

  • No account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • The app contains no third-party SDKs.
  • The developer collects no data about you or how you use the app.
  • What you create stays on your device and – if you use iCloud – in your own private iCloud.

This policy applies to the Kolorist app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, developed by Eivind Arnstein Johansen (“the developer”). It explains what data the app handles, where it is stored and who can access it.

Data collected

None. The app does not communicate with any servers run by the developer and sends no data to the developer. The app uses no analytics tools, third-party crash reporting, advertising or tracking, and no data is shared with anyone.

Data stored on your device and in your iCloud

Palettes and saved colours

Palettes, saved single colours and the names you give them are stored on your device. If you’re signed in to iCloud, they are also synced through your private iCloud database (CloudKit). The private database is tied to your Apple Account, and only you can access it – not the developer.

ICC profiles

ICC profiles you import are stored in the app’s iCloud Drive folder (Kolorist › Profiler) when iCloud Drive is available, otherwise on the device only. The folder is visible to you in Files and Finder.

Settings

Your in-app settings are stored locally on the device.

Storage and sync through iCloud are handled by Apple and are subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Permissions the app asks for

Camera

The camera is only used live on the device while you pick colours or view your surroundings through the colour vision filter. No photos or videos are stored or sent anywhere. The app asks for permission the first time, and you can revoke it at any time in system settings.

Photos

The app only gets access to the images you choose yourself in the system photo picker. Images are processed on the device to pick colours and find dominant colours, and are not stored or sent.

Clipboard

The app writes to the clipboard when you copy a colour or a palette, and reads from it when you paste.

Apple Intelligence

The AI features – such as creating a palette from value words, describing a colour, adjusting with free text, naming colours and critiquing palettes – use Apple’s on-device language model. What you type is processed on the device and is not sent to the developer. When Apple Intelligence isn’t available, the app uses a built-in knowledge base of colour concepts, also on the device.

Siri and Shortcuts

When you use the app’s actions through Siri or Shortcuts, the request is handled by Apple’s system. The developer receives nothing of what you say or type. Apple’s processing is subject to Apple’s own terms and privacy policy.

How to delete your data

  • Delete palettes and colours in the app. The change syncs to your other devices through iCloud.
  • Remove ICC profiles in the app, or delete the files from the Kolorist › Profiler folder in iCloud Drive.
  • Deleting the app removes the data stored locally on the device. You can manage data in iCloud under iCloud in your Apple Account settings.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Should the app ever start handling data differently from what is described here, the policy will be updated before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to Eivind Arnstein Johansen at eivind.johansen@ntnu.no.

This policy is available in Norwegian (norsk) and English.
